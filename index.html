Pay the loan early?

Ask your bank for the loan's real yearly rate (APR, not the "flat" rate) and the early-payoff fee. If the APR is above about 20%, paying it down early beats every safe investment, because every pound you repay earns that rate with no risk. Card installments on ValU or Tru at 0% should not be paid early. Tell me the rate and I will redo the numbers.
